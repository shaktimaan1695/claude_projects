# Deceptively Hard Exercises

*Looks simple. Destroys you.*

-----

## LOWER BODY

**Pistol Squat** — Single-leg strength + balance + ankle mobility. Doubles load on one leg.

> 🎥 [Squat University - Pistol Squat Fix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDcniqddTeE)

**Nordic Hamstring Curl** — Brutal eccentric hamstring load. Reduces hamstring injury risk ~51%.

> 🎥 [E3 Rehab - Nordic Curl Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BnEFTlEE8OA)

**Walking Lunge (3s eccentric)** — Slow eccentric magnifies quad/glute demand. Metabolic stress spikes.

> 🎥 [Jeff Nippard - Lunge Technique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L8fvypPrzzs)

**Shrimp Squat** — Like pistol but leg behind you. Hammers glutes more than pistol.

> 🎥 [FitnessFAQs - Shrimp Squat Progression](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1kOSy0gOHSk)

-----

## HINGE / POSTERIOR CHAIN

**Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift** — Stability demand is humbling even unloaded. Exposes L/R imbalances.

> 🎥 [Mind Pump TV - Single Leg RDL](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_PR7FNbDkJU)

**Glute-Ham Raise** — Full posterior chain under load. Most people can't do 1 clean rep.

> 🎥 [Calgary Barbell - GHR Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCdHCxBJ7DQ)

**Reverse Nordic Curl** — Targets rectus femoris eccentrically. Looks like nothing, quads catch fire.

> 🎥 [Meghan Callaway - Reverse Nordic Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=itG5_8fFD0Y)

-----

## UPPER BODY

**Ring Dips** — Instability makes a 'basic' dip exponentially harder. Full upper body.

> 🎥 [K Squared Fitness - Ring Dip Progression](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2bnGMIJOBaA)

**Archer Push-Ups** — Unilateral push-up progression. One arm does ~80% of the work.

> 🎥 [Calisthenicmovement - Archer Push-Up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oy6YKDkTTvM)

**Turkish Get-Up** — Coordination + stability across every joint. Full-body integration.

> 🎥 [StrongFirst - TGU Tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0bWRPC49-KI)

**Face Pulls (3s hold)** — Rear delts give out fast. Critical for shoulder health + posture.

> 🎥 [Jeff Cavaliere - Face Pull Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rep-qVOkqgk)

-----

## CORE

**Ab Wheel Rollout** — Full extension from standing is elite-level. Anti-extension king.

> 🎥 [Jeff Nippard - Ab Wheel Progression](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rqiTPl0U0FE)

**Pallof Press (slow rotation)** — Anti-rotation. Core has to resist force in all planes.

> 🎥 [ATHLEAN-X - Pallof Press Variations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AH_QZLm_0-s)

**Copenhagen Plank** — Adductor + oblique demand is sneaky. Groin injury prevention.

> 🎥 [E3 Rehab - Copenhagen Plank](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mYErhBOqd0)

**Dead Bug (band resistance)** — Anti-extension + coordination. Harder than it looks by orders of magnitude.

> 🎥 [Squat University - Dead Bug](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I5xbsA71v1A)

-----

## FULL BODY / CONDITIONING

**Bear Crawl (loaded/slow)** — Loaded or slow = metabolic nightmare. Shoulders, core, hips all working.

> 🎥 [Onnit - Bear Crawl Variations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=USHGBRhSZEY)

**Farmer's Carry** — Grip, traps, core, gait. The simplest hard exercise that exists.

> 🎥 [Alan Thrall - Farmer's Walk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rt17lmnaLSM)

**Sled Push (low handles)** — Zero eccentric = less soreness but devastating metabolic cost.

> 🎥 [Garage Gym Reviews - Sled Training](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aFGnMbxQKFo)

-----

## BONUS

**Wall Sit (90°)** — Isometric quad endurance. Most people gas out well before they predict.

> 🎥 [Livestrong - Wall Sit Form](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y-wV4Lz-g_o)

**Tempo Training (4-0-1-0)** — 60% of max at 4s eccentric feels harder than 85% at normal speed.

> 🎥 [Renaissance Periodization - Tempo Training](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VB6zOJ55n3g)

-----

*Pro tip: Try 4-0-1-0 tempo on exercises you already do at 60% max. Disproportionate hypertrophy + stability gains.*
